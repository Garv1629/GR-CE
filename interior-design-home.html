Contemplative Private Residences

Contemplative private residences celebrating quietude, natural daylight, monolithic limestone volumes, and bespoke joinery. An architectural sanctuary designed for contemplative modern living and enduring privacy.

Discipline Private Residential Architecture
Atelier Grèce Interiors
Materiality Honed Limestone, European Oak & Lime Plaster
Scope Complete Architectural Interior & Millwork
Grèce Private Residence Archive
Residential Philosophy
“A private residence should feel like an architectural retreat from the noise of the world — where natural daylight is the primary ornamentation.”

In our residential commissions, architecture is treated as a calming framework for everyday life. We reject visual clutter in favor of continuous architectural volumes, seamless floor-to-ceiling millwork, and balanced sightlines.

The palette is grounded in warm mineral neutrals: honed limestone, soft chalk washes, raw linens, and hand-rubbed oak. These tactile materials age with dignity and respond intimately to shifting solar angles throughout the day.

Every furniture piece is custom-commissioned from the atelier or carefully curated to sit in silent alignment with the architectural perimeter.
