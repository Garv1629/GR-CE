03 // MONOGRAPH Hospitality Architecture // Heritage Dining

Rasoi — Heritage Spatial Dining & Tactile Joinery

A rich sensory ode to culinary heritage: sculpted terracotta-rendered arches, hand-turned solid teakwood millwork, and warm amber illumination evoking timeless domestic comfort.

Project Rasoi Dining Environment
Category Heritage Hospitality Interior
Materiality Terracotta Plaster, Smoked Teak & Cast Brass
Scope Spatial Architecture & Custom Atelier Millwork
Photography // Piyush Macrography for Grèce
Spatial Narrative
“Heritage is not replication; it is the distillation of emotional warmth into honest, enduring materials that gather memory over time.”

For Rasoi, Grèce was invited to design a spatial environment that honors deep culinary traditions without lapsing into nostalgia. The response is a modern architectural choreography grounded in honest craftsmanship and authentic earth pigments.

Walls are finished in custom-formulated terracotta lime wash, creating velvety textured surfaces that absorb sound and radiate a warm glow under low-lux lighting.

Custom smoked teak banquettes and brass-jointed dining tables provide tactile reassurance, while carved plaster niches celebrate handcrafted artifacts collected across regional artisan workshops.

Fig. 3.1 — Custom Teakwood Banquettes. Hand-rubbed oil finish paired with woven tactile seat cushions.
Fig. 3.2 — Recessed Wall Niche. Hand-troweled lime wash framing cast-brass functional artifacts.
Fig. 3.3 — Luminaire Composition. Low-voltage brass fixtures generating pools of warm intimacy.
Fig. 3.4 — Material Authenticity. Natural wood grains responding to warm candlelight.
