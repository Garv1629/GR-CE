Aahii — Sculptural Dining Architecture

An intimate culinary destination defined by an undulating timber ceiling installation, concealed perimeter lighting, and tactile booth architecture sculpted in hand-hewn dark oak.

Project Aahii Fine Dining
Category Hospitality Interior Architecture
Materiality Dark Oak, Spun Brass & Basalt Stone
Scope Complete Spatial Design & Custom Millwork
Photography // YnotUS for Grèce
Spatial Narrative
“At Aahii, the ceiling does not enclose the space; it becomes a floating canopy of rhythmic timber fins that diffuses light and absorbs sound.”

Commissioned to craft a singular sensory identity for Aahii, the Grèce atelier conceived an architectural environment where spatial geometry takes precedence over superficial decoration.

The defining intervention is a masterfully calculated suspended timber lattice. Over three hundred custom CNC-milled oak fins contour the ceiling plane, establishing an undulating topographical flow that guides the eye toward intimate dining pockets.

Ambient luminaires are concealed within the ceiling reveals, washing down textured lime walls and reflecting softly across hand-waxed dining surfaces to curate an atmosphere of hushed revelry.

Fig. 1.0 — Suspended Timber Canopy. Custom oak fins sculpted to modulate acoustics and integrate low-voltage illumination.
Fig. 1.1 — Intimate Alcove Banquettes. Custom fluted upholstery and integrated low-glare wall sconces.
Fig. 1.2 — Column Reveal. Shadowline joint and indirect upward wash highlighting tactile mineral textures.
Fig. 1.3 — Custom Atelier Dining Seating. Ergonomic woodcraft with woven tactile upholstery.
Fig. 1.4 — Central Dining Composition. Axial view balancing the dark timber floor and luminous ceiling canopy.
