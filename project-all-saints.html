All Saints — Monolithic Dining & Bar Sanctuary

A cathedral-like dining sanctuary sculpted through rhythmic stone arches, a monolithic curved bar counter, and warm indirect luminescence casting soft gradients across textured plaster.

Project All Saints Dining & Lounge
Category Hospitality Spatial Architecture
Materiality Carved Travertine, Fluted Ash & Antique Bronze
Scope Arched Hall Architecture, Bar Counter & Lighting
Photography // Architectural Archive for Grèce
Spatial Narrative
“We sought to create a timeless architectural sanctuary: where classical vaulted arches are reinterpreted with modernist restraint and tactile mineral permanence.”

All Saints was designed as a sanctuary of hospitality. Inspired by historic cloisters and monolithic stone masonry, the architecture unfolds along a sequence of vaulted portals that frame sightlines throughout the multi-tiered dining volume.

The centerpiece of the establishment is an expansive, curved monolithic bar counter executed in honed travertine. Rather than a separate furnishing, the bar arises organically from the floor plane, grounding the lively energy of the social room.

Light fixtures are concealed behind architectural arch returns and low-level pedestal bases, transforming the structural envelope into a luminous sculpture that changes temperament between dusk and late night.
